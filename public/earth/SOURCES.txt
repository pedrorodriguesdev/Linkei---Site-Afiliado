Earth visual assets

Geographic day and night texture source: NASA imagery distributed in the three-globe example assets:
https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe/tree/master/example/img
Original files: earth-blue-marble.jpg and earth-night.jpg.

Cloud layer source:
https://github.com/turban/webgl-earth/blob/master/images/fair_clouds_4k.png

The static transparent Earth images were generated from an orthographic spherical projection of the same geographic textures, with directional sunlight, real night-light data, cloud opacity and atmospheric shading. They are not product artwork.

Mobile: earth-mobile.webp (static only).
Desktop fallback: earth-static.webp.
Desktop WebGL sphere: day.webp, night.webp and clouds.webp.
